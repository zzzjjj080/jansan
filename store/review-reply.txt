3. FUNCTION AND TARGET AUDIENCE
Jansan is a score sheet for the tile game mahjong.
Problem: when friends play mahjong, someone must write each round's scores on paper or type them into an app. Existing tools need many taps per round, which interrupts the game.
How it works: players are columns, rounds are rows. The user taps a cell and types a score. Because the scores in one round always add up to zero, entering three players' scores lets the app derive the fourth automatically. Entering two digits confirms the value and advances to the next player with no confirm button. Five or six people can rotate through a four-player table, and the app marks sitting-out players automatically.
Audience: people who play mahjong recreationally and want a running score sheet during the game.
IMPORTANT: this app records point scores only. It contains NO gambling, NO betting, NO wagering of real or virtual currency, and NO simulated gambling. There is no money feature of any kind. It is a record keeper, comparable to a scorecard for a card game or a bowling score sheet.

4. SETUP AND ACCESS
No setup, account, or login credentials are required. Every feature is available immediately after launch. No sample files are needed.
- Launch the app. A table with four sample player names appears.
- Tap any empty cell. The numeric keypad appears.
- Enter a number. Two digits confirm automatically and the cursor advances. The red minus key makes the value negative.
- After three players' scores are entered, the fourth fills in automatically so the round totals zero.
- Chart icon (top bar): rank statistics and cumulative score graph.
- Share icon: the table as CSV text, which can be copied or shared.
- Gear icon: participating members (up to six), 3/4-player presets, decimal mode, appearance, saved records, new session.
- Tap the round number at the left edge of a row to delete that round.
