3. APP FUNCTION AND TARGET AUDIENCE

Jansan is a score sheet for the tile game mahjong.

Problem it solves:
When friends play mahjong together, someone has to write down each round's
point scores on paper or type them into an app. Existing tools require many
taps per round, which interrupts the game.

What the app does:
Players are listed as columns and rounds as rows. The user taps a cell and
types a point score. Because the scores in one round always add up to zero,
entering three players' scores lets the app derive the fourth automatically.
Entering two digits confirms the value and moves to the next player without
pressing a confirm button. Five or six people can rotate through a four-player
table, and the app marks the sitting-out players automatically.

Target audience:
People who play mahjong recreationally with friends or family and want to keep
a running score sheet during the game.

Important clarification:
This app records point scores only. It contains NO gambling, NO betting,
NO wagering of money or virtual currency, and NO simulated gambling.
There is no money or currency feature of any kind in the app.
It is a calculator and record keeper, comparable to a scorecard for a
card game or a bowling score sheet.


4. HOW TO SET UP AND ACCESS THE MAIN FEATURES

No setup, no account, and no login credentials are required.
All features are available immediately after launching the app.

- Launch the app. A score table with four sample player names is shown.
- Tap any empty cell in the table. The numeric keypad appears.
- Enter a number. Two digits are confirmed automatically and the cursor
  advances to the next player. Use the red minus key for negative scores.
- After three players' scores are entered, the fourth is filled in
  automatically so that the round totals zero.
- Tap the chart icon in the top bar to see rank statistics and a cumulative
  score graph.
- Tap the share icon to see the table as CSV text, which can be copied
  or shared.
- Tap the gear icon for settings: participating members (up to six),
  three or four player presets, decimal mode, appearance, saved records,
  and starting a new session.
- Tap the round number at the left edge of a row to delete that round.

No sample files are required.
